Services / A/C & Heating

Cold in August, warm in January.

A/C that blows warm usually means a leak, and recharging without finding it just means paying again next summer. We find the leak, fix it, then recharge to spec.

Common questions

Can you just top off my A/C?

We can recharge, but we'll test for leaks first and tell you what we find. A system that's low has a leak somewhere.

Why does my heater only work when I'm driving?

Often low coolant or a failing water pump or thermostat. It's worth checking soon, since the same problem can overheat the engine.

Do you service the new refrigerant?

Yes. We're equipped for R-1234yf, which most vehicles built after about 2017 use.
