Common questions

How often should I align?

Any time you install new tires, after hitting a serious pothole or curb, or if the car pulls or the wheel sits off-center. Annually is a good habit in Oregon.

Do you sell tires?

Yes. Tell us how you drive and we'll quote options at a few price points. We can usually have them the next day.

Do you fix flats?

Yes, if the puncture is in the repairable area of the tread. We'll show you a photo if it isn't.
