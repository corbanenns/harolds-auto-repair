About Harold's Quality Auto Repair

Your Trusted Partner in Auto Care

Since 1996, Harold's Quality Auto Repair has been the go-to auto repair shop for Salem residents who value quality, honesty, and exceptional service. Our commitment to excellence has made us a cornerstone of the West Salem community.

We've recently expanded to a state-of-the-art 8,000 sq ft facility with 9 lifts and 11 bays, equipped with the latest diagnostic technology. Our team of ASE Master Certified technicians brings decades of combined experience to every repair.

As a BBB Accredited business and NAPA AutoCare Center, we stand behind our work with a comprehensive 1 year, 12,000 mile warranty. When you choose Harold's, you're choosing peace of mind.
