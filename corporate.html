Corporate programs

Car care as a company benefit.

Three programs for West Salem employers: corporate vehicle accounts, an employee car-care program that costs the company nothing, and priority scheduling with text approvals so nobody loses a workday to a repair.

Corporate vehicle accounts

Company cars, executive vehicles, pool cars and sales fleets. Net-30 terms for established accounts, one itemized statement a month, a named service advisor, and a quarterly vehicle-health summary built from the inspection records.

Employee Car Care Program

Free for the employer to offer. Your employees show a badge or pay stub and get 10% off labor, priority scheduling, free seasonal safety inspections and the same photo-inspection transparency as every customer. We'll supply a one-page flyer for your break room and an intro for your HR newsletter.

Priority scheduling & approvals your way

Business vehicles go in first thing in the morning, with same-day turnaround where the repair allows. Set a pre-approval limit per vehicle or have every estimate texted to one person, and pay by text so nobody leaves their desk. We don't run a shuttle, so plan a second driver for drop-off.

Why companies choose Harold's

  • Documentation. Every visit's photos, measurements and approvals saved per vehicle. Useful for insurance, resale and for the person who signs the invoices.
  • Predictable costs. Deferred items are tracked and scheduled, so a $180 brake job doesn't become a $900 rotor-and-caliper job six months later.
  • Less downtime. Priority scheduling, same-day turnaround where the repair allows, and 11 bays so several units can be in at once.
  • One point of contact. A named advisor who knows your vehicles and your approval rules.
  • A real warranty. 1 year / 12,000 miles, honored at NAPA AutoCare Centers nationwide for vehicles that travel.
