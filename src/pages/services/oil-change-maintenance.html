Common questions

How long does an oil change take?

Usually 45 minutes to an hour including the inspection. You're welcome to wait, or drop off and we'll text you when it's ready.

Do you use the oil my manufacturer specifies?

Yes. We match the viscosity and spec in your owner's manual, including European long-life oils.

What's a 60k service?

The bundle of items your manufacturer calls for around 60,000 miles: typically fluids, filters, spark plugs on some engines, and a thorough inspection. We quote it from the factory schedule for your exact vehicle and skip anything already done.
