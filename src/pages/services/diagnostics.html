Common questions

How much is a diagnostic?

We charge a flat diagnostic fee that covers the testing time to find the cause. You'll get the result, the evidence and a written estimate before any repair.

My light is on but the car drives fine. Is it urgent?

A steady light usually isn't an emergency but shouldn't be ignored; a flashing light means stop driving and call us. Either way, we'll tell you straight.

Can you just clear the code?

We can, but it will come back if the cause isn't fixed, and clearing it erases the data we need to diagnose it.
