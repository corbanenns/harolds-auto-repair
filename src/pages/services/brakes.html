Common questions

How do I know if I need brakes?

Squealing, grinding, a pulsing pedal, a longer stop or a brake warning light. Or ask us at your next oil change; pad thickness is on every inspection.

Pads only, or pads and rotors?

It depends on rotor thickness and condition, which we measure. If the rotors are within spec and true, we'll tell you pads alone are fine.

Can I bring my own parts?

We install parts we source so we can warranty the job. We'll match quality to your budget.
