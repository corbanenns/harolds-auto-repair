Common questions

Why does my car sound louder all of a sudden?

Usually a rusted-through pipe or a cracked flex joint. It's often a small weld repair rather than a whole new system. We'll show you a photo.

Can you make it sound a certain way?

Yes. Tell us what you're after, deeper tone, quieter, or a specific muffler brand, and we'll build it.

Catalytic converter was stolen. Can you help?

Yes, and we can add a shield to make it harder next time. We'll help with the insurance paperwork.
