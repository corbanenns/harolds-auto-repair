Digital inspections

No surprises. No pressure. See what we see.

You shouldn't have to take a mechanic's word for it. Every vehicle at Harold's gets a digital multi-point inspection, and the full report, photos included, lands on your phone before any work is authorized.

We inspect, with a camera in hand

Your technician works through a multi-point checklist on a tablet in the bay: brakes, tires, fluids, belts, battery, suspension, lights, leaks. Anything worn gets a photo or a short video and a note in plain English.

You get a text link

The report goes to your phone as soon as it's done. Each item is rated green (good), yellow (monitor) or red (needs attention), with the measurement that backs it up: pad thickness in millimeters, tread depth in 32nds, battery voltage.

You approve only what you want

Tap approve or decline on each line. Ask a question right in the thread. Nothing starts until you've okayed it, and declined items stay on your vehicle's record so we can remind you when they're actually due.

What's on the inspection

  • Brake pads and rotors, measured in mm, with photos
  • Tire tread depth on all four, tire age and pressure
  • Battery load test and charging system
  • Engine oil, coolant, brake, transmission and power steering fluid condition
  • Belts, hoses and visible leaks
  • Suspension, steering and CV joints
  • Lights, wipers, cabin and engine air filters
  • Check-engine and stored trouble codes

The inspection is included with every oil change and most services. A standalone safety inspection is available if you just want a look.

Why we do it this way

Trust is the whole business

A photo of a cracked belt ends the "do I really need this?" conversation. You decide with the same information we have.

Yellow means yellow

Not everything needs fixing today. We tell you what can wait and roughly how long, and we follow up when it's actually due instead of upselling now.

Your history, on file

Every inspection, photo and invoice stays attached to your vehicle. Selling the car? Ask us for the full record.

Common questions

Does the inspection cost extra?

No. It's included with oil changes and most repairs. A standalone safety inspection has a small fee that's waived if you have the work done here.

What if I don't want texts?

No problem. We can email the report or walk you through it at the counter or by phone. Texting is just the fastest.

Will you pressure me on the yellow items?

No. Yellow means "monitor." We note it, you decide, and we remind you when it's getting close. Many customers plan yellow items for their next oil change.

Can I see the photos later?

Yes. The link stays active, and the report is saved to your vehicle's record at the shop.
